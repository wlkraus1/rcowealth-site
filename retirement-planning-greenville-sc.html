Client Process

A clear path from question to recommendation.

The goal is not to overwhelm you with products. It is to identify what actually needs attention, explain the tradeoffs, and document practical next steps.

  • Identify expected spending, income sources, and timing needs.
  • Review current accounts, allocation, liquidity, and risk exposure.
  • Consider life insurance, survivor needs, and family obligations where relevant.
  • Create a practical review rhythm as markets and life change.
