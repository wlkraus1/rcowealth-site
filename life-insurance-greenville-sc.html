What Rae & Co helps with

  • Existing policies and employer coverage
  • New term and permanent coverage needs
  • Income replacement and debt exposure
  • Dependents, education, and family obligations
  • Business-owner, buy-sell, or key-person risk
  • Carrier fit, underwriting path, ownership, beneficiaries, liquidity, and estate coordination

What clients should leave with

  • A clear coverage strategy tied to the financial plan
  • A gap and overlap summary
  • Carrier, underwriting, and application next steps when new coverage makes sense
  • Replacement or reduction considerations before changing existing coverage

How this connects

Rae & Co treats this work as part of a broader financial architecture. Portfolio, retirement income, insurance, cash flow, taxes, family obligations, and business exposure should be reviewed together instead of handled as disconnected projects.
