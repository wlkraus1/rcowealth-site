Policy Fit

Term vs. permanent is not the first question.

Term and permanent insurance can both have a place. The better first question is: what job should the policy do?

  • Review whether employer coverage is enough.
  • Check beneficiaries, ownership, and coverage amount.
  • Compare policy type to the actual planning goal.
  • Consider family, business, retirement, and legacy needs together.
