Life Insurance Review

Life insurance should protect real obligations, not a random number.

This section borrows the best idea from Ethos/Policygenius style education without making Rae & Co sound like a quote marketplace.

What is the policy supposed to do?

Replace income, cover debt, support family obligations, provide business continuity, or address a legacy/liquidity need.

Does your current coverage still fit?

Review amount, term length, ownership, beneficiary, premium, policy type, and whether life changes created a gap.

What changed since you bought it?

Marriage, children, home purchase, divorce, business ownership, new debt, retirement timing, or job changes can all matter.

What should happen next?

Keep it, adjust it, replace it only if appropriate, or document why no change is needed. No product-first pitch.
