Why a worksheet

Because "2x salary from work" was never your number.

Employer group coverage is a benefit HR sized for everyone, and it usually ends when the job does. The worksheet adds up what your family would actually need: debts cleared, income replaced for the years that matter, the kids covered, minus what you already have. What is left is your gap. Then you can make a real decision.

Step 1

Add up the need

Debts, income replacement years, and goals for the kids, in plain numbers you already know.

Step 2

Subtract what you have

Savings, existing policies, and the coverage through work, counted honestly.

Step 3

See your gap

One number. If you want a second set of eyes on it, a free 20-minute review is available. No pitch.
