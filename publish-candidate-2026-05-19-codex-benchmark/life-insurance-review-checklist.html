Rae & Co Capital Checklist

Life insurance review checklist.

Use these questions to organize your current coverage, family obligations, business needs, and next-step questions before a protection planning review.

Request a Review

Educational only. This checklist is not individualized insurance, investment, tax, or legal advice.

1. What job should the policy do?

  1. Replace household income for a specific number of years?
  2. Pay off mortgage, business debt, or education obligations?
  3. Create liquidity for estate, tax, or family transition needs?
  4. Protect a business partner, key employee, or buy-sell agreement?

2. What coverage already exists?

  1. Employer group coverage amount and portability if employment changes.
  2. Individual term or permanent policies, including owner, insured, and beneficiary.
  3. Policy duration, premium schedule, riders, and any cash value or loan activity.

3. Who depends on the coverage?

  1. Spouse, children, parents, business partners, employees, or lenders.
  2. Approximate annual income need and how long that need may last.
  3. Major obligations: mortgage, childcare, education, healthcare, debt, or business continuity.

4. What could make coverage harder later?

  1. Health changes, age, occupation, travel, hobbies, or underwriting history.
  2. Budget constraints or uncertainty about permanent vs. term structure.
  3. Upcoming life changes: marriage, birth, business growth, retirement, or debt changes.

5. What should be reviewed before applying?

  1. Coverage amount and duration.
  2. Policy type and funding commitment.
  3. Beneficiary and ownership structure.
  4. Carrier strength, underwriting path, and expected timeline.

6. What should you avoid sending through a website form?

  • Social Security numbers, account numbers, policy numbers, medical records, or detailed financial statements.
  • Use the form for a high-level question. Rae & Co Capital can request sensitive documents through a more appropriate channel if needed.
