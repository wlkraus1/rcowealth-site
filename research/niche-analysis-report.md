# The Virtual Wealth Advisory Benchmark Report

### How the best fee-only and virtual advisory firms win online — and where Rae & Co Capital can lead

*Prepared by the Rae & Co Capital strategy team · June 2026*

---

## Why this matters

Wealth management is going virtual. The firms growing fastest today — Facet, Domain Money, Empower, Zoe, Savvy — serve clients entirely through a screen, and they're winning the trust of households that would never walk into a downtown office. The opportunity for a credentialed, fiduciary, **South Carolina-based virtual firm** has never been larger. But the bar for the *website* has risen with it. For a virtual firm, the website isn't a brochure — it *is* the lobby, the first handshake, and the proof of competence, all at once.

We studied eight of the strongest firms in the category to define the benchmark and find Rae & Co's edge.

---

## The industry benchmark — what "good" looks like in 2026

Across the leading firms, four traits show up again and again:

**1. Radical fee transparency.** The category default is to hide pricing. The leaders do the opposite — they put their flat-fee model on the homepage. "No AUM percentage. No commissions. No hidden fees." Showing the price *builds* trust rather than scaring people off.

**2. Proof in numbers, not adjectives.** The best sites replace "personalized, holistic, trusted" with hard figures: Domain Money promises clients *2–4x more wealth by retirement* and *350+ hours saved a year*; Facet cites *24,000+ members* and a *#1 ranking from USA TODAY*; Savvy leads with *$5B managed, 100+ advisors*. Specificity reads as competence.

**3. One clear next step.** Winning sites drive every visitor toward a single, low-pressure, high-value action — "Free Strategy Session," "Get Started," "Find an Advisor" — repeated throughout, never a vague "Contact Us."

**4. A visible trust stack.** Fiduciary status, SEC/state registration, CFP® credentials, a recognized custodian (Schwab, Fidelity), SIPC protection, and bank-grade security — presented cleanly and prominently, not buried in a footer.

## What the top firms do that average firms don't

| The top 10% | The average firm |
|---|---|
| Shows the price, frames it as fairness | Hides fees, says "contact us for pricing" |
| Quantifies outcomes (+3% returns, 2–4x wealth) | Vague promises ("comprehensive, personalized") |
| One repeated, consultative call-to-action | Multiple competing buttons, generic "Contact" |
| Layered proof: experts + dated reviews + press | One stale logo bar, if anything |
| Names the custodian and credentials up front | Trust signals hidden or absent |
| Restrained, intentional motion and whitespace | Cluttered, stock-photo handshakes |
| Self-qualifying "people like me" content | One-size-fits-all messaging |

## The gap in the market — Rae & Co's opening

Here's what our review revealed: **no leading firm owns the "premium, boutique, private-office" aesthetic.** Facet and Domain Money feel like friendly fintech. Wealthfront and Betterment feel like apps. Savvy is the closest to a sophisticated navy palette but still reads tech-startup. The robo-advisors deliberately *remove* the human; Betterment even sells "access to a human advisor" as a paid *premium* upgrade.

That validates Rae & Co's entire thesis:

> **The human, credentialed relationship is the premium product — and Rae & Co can deliver it virtually, wrapped in a brand that actually looks premium.**

Navy and gold, elegant serif headlines, generous whitespace, and a calm, confident tone signal "private wealth office" — a look the accessible-fintech crowd has left wide open.

## Recommended approach for the rebuild

- **Hero:** an elegant serif headline expressing the core promise — *"Premium advice. No office visit required."* — with a confident value sub-line and one CTA, over a navy ground with restrained gold accents.
- **Value props:** three to four, each carrying a number or concrete outcome (retirement income, investment management, planning, protection).
- **Trust band:** Schwab custody · SEC/SC registration · CFP® fiduciary · secure & encrypted — as a clean, prominent module.
- **Proof:** named/credentialed voice + dated, disclosure-compliant client testimonials + any press or ratings (with required dates and disclosures).
- **Self-qualifying paths:** "nearing retirement," "business owner / professional," "recently inherited or rolled over a 401(k)."
- **One CTA everywhere:** *Book Your Intro Call.* Low pressure, high value, consultative.
- **Motion & feel:** subtle parallax, thin gold-line reveals, cinematic but quiet — premium, never busy. Mobile-first throughout.

> Get this right and Rae & Co won't just match the category leaders — it will look more trustworthy than firms ten times its size, and own a position none of them have claimed: **the boutique virtual wealth office.**
