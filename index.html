Virtual by design

The firm is virtual because the client’s life is not built around an advisor’s office.

A premium virtual model should feel easier, tighter, and more intentional — not distant. The relationship is built around access, clarity, and the ability to make important decisions from wherever life and business are happening.

Access

Meet without geography becoming the constraint.

Intro calls, planning reviews, and follow-up conversations are handled virtually so clients can stay focused on the decision, not the commute.

Workflow

Keep the work organized between meetings.

The relationship is structured around clear next steps, document review, implementation items, and review rhythm.

Portals

Use provider access where it belongs.

Investment account access stays with Schwab. Insurance account access stays with Mutual of Omaha. Rae & Co does not collect portal credentials.

Review

Stay current without waiting for an annual office visit.

Markets, income, business obligations, family needs, and coverage questions change. The model is designed for regular review, not once-a-year theater.
